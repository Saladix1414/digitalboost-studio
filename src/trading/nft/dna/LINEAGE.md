# NFT DNA Lineage

Lineage records version-to-version DNA changes.

It tracks:

- sequence
- prior entry reference
- identity snapshot hash
- integrity snapshot hash
- blockchain proof snapshot hash
- change reason
- timestamp

Lineage is historical evidence only.

It does not establish:

- creator provenance
- ownership history
- legal authenticity
- blockchain finality
- governance authority

Those concerns belong to later Origin and Provenance phases.
