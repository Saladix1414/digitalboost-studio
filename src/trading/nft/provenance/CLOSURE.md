## Important boundary

A successful closure means the modeled evidence is internally consistent.

It does not establish:

- legal ownership
- authorship authority
- absolute authenticity
- real-world identity
- governance approval
- blockchain finality beyond the evidence already represented

Closure remains:

`authoritative:false`
