# NFT Detail Evidence Composition

Phase 28C composes cross-domain evidence inside NFT Detail.

Displayed evidence may include:

- Origin record
- DNA identity
- DNA integrity
- blockchain proof reference
- provenance history

## Consistency

Detail also evaluates:

- asset alignment
- collection alignment
- provenance closure validity

These checks are informational.

They do not establish:

- legal ownership
- absolute authenticity
- seller authority
- payment
- blockchain finality
- governance authority

Evidence presence != truth.

Consistency != authority.

Blockchain proof != legal title.

All composed evidence remains:

`authoritative:false`
