DigitalBoost Studio and its original source code are proprietary work.

publish, sublicense, sell or commercially exploit the original
DigitalBoost source code.

All rights are reserved by the current project owner.

A future legal entity may become the owner of the intellectual property
through a documented assignment.

## Repository status

This repository is intended to remain private.

Private repository access does not grant ownership or an intellectual
property license.

Access may be provided only for authorized development, review,
investment diligence, security review or other explicitly approved
purposes.

---

## Third-party software

DigitalBoost uses third-party dependencies.

Those dependencies remain governed by their respective licenses.

This proprietary policy applies only to original DigitalBoost work and
does not override third-party license terms.

---

## Secrets

Credentials, API keys, access tokens, passwords, private keys and other
secrets must never be committed to the repository.

Real secrets must be stored outside source control through approved
environment or secret-management mechanisms.

`.env.example` may contain names and non-sensitive example values only.

---

## AI-generated and assisted work

AI-assisted development does not automatically establish provenance,
ownership or licensing of third-party material.

Generated or externally sourced code must be reviewed before inclusion
when licensing, attribution or ownership is uncertain.

---

## External contributions

No external contributor should be granted write access or have code
merged into DigitalBoost until contribution ownership and licensing
terms are defined.

Future contributors, contractors or employees may require an
appropriate intellectual-property assignment or contribution agreement.

---

## Fundraising and due diligence

Access granted to an investor, accelerator, advisor or reviewer does not
grant rights to reproduce or commercialize DigitalBoost source code.

Sensitive technical material should be shared according to its
relevance to the diligence process.
